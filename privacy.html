Privacy policy

Lilah is a personal assistant app with one user, its owner.

  • What it accesses: calendar events on calendars the owner has shared with the app's Google account, so it can remind the owner of upcoming events and add events the owner asks for.
  • Where data goes: data is processed only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared with, or sent to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Retention: only what's needed for reminders is kept, on the owner's computer.
  • Revoking access: the owner can remove access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.

Last updated: October 26, 2026.
